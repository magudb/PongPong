## Places and calendar

Trætoppen is the settlement. Hyggebyen is its shopping district. Min Trækronsgård is the farm on its own branch. Mørkeskoven is the forest below. The race name is Halvling / Hobbit. Calendar and weather words resolve through UI_WEEKDAY, UI_MONTH and UI_WEATHER keys.
