# Relationships, wedding and life at home

The eleven documented adult candidates now support optional heartfriend, engaged and married stages. There is no gender field, jealousy, breakup or children. Hero twins remain absent. The two crafted tokens use an explicit special-gift path: the picker explains the item, ignores ordinary gift allowances, defaults to keeping the friendship, rechecks eligibility before consuming anything, and commits the item and relationship together before inventory observers run. A refusal or cancellation costs nothing. Several heartfriends are allowed; only one engagement is allowed. Hoppe, Brumme, Maja and Birk have individual short scenes; the other seven use archetype scenes. The shared event runtime supplies visible staging, skip and cleanup.

An engagement needs seven days as heartfriends, ten hearts and a tier-three home. The announced wedding is the first Sunday at least seven days later with no fixed festival on that day or either adjacent date, across season/year boundaries. It is added to the calendar without making a tenth annual fixed festival. Coming to the fountain starts the staged wedding; completing or intentionally skipping it commits marriage once. Missing 20:00 moves the wedding to another safe Sunday without consuming another token. The event completion day is checked when committing.

The partner retains their job. Home visits use the baked route table and retain every work/service interval. The morning departure includes the documented double-walk/five-minute margin; no visit is invented when that would precede 06:00. The current routes are longer than the original design examples: Monday departures include Sofus at about 08:21 and Postbud Pip at about 07:24. Brumme has no Monday morning home window with these routes. Evening visits fit available time between 19:00 and 22:00. `partner-routes.json` contains all eleven candidates, seven weekdays and two weather variants. These are deterministic schedule checks, not play telemetry.

The daily helper waters at most twelve planted crops, otherwise feeds hungry animals using real accepted food from the backpack, otherwise leaves a note. All affected plots/animals and the saved daily stamp commit before observers run. It does not repeat on load. Weekly visits name eligible four-heart friends; saved per-week history prevents a newly befriended citizen from causing a repeat. The actual visit and attempt day now survive loading, so the same-day guest remains present and the gift cannot be replayed. Tier-eight parties invite up to six named friends. Disposable room actors appear when the real schedule permits; they retain reachable, separated positions after dialogue and furniture changes. Named visits and parties now carry real unlock status.

The live routine planner previously ignored the festival override's `until` value. It now honours early departures. Attending keepers with evening duties also receive a measured return margin, retaining any larger authored margin. Tests verify actual counter positions after all nine fixed festivals and the wedding, plus shop availability throughout the wedding day. Ordinary routine markers are hidden during staged scenes; explicit scene emotes remain available.

Home regression fixtures now account for starting wardrobe perks and existing starter furniture, and explicitly model a full furniture store. They still check exact tier bonuses, deferred gifts and observer safety. The obsolete social-data skip now checks the shipped authored citizen/taste tables, while fallback tests explicitly remove those tables.
