AudioManager owns eight SFX players, two ambience players, rain, music and voice; there is no new autoload or 3D player. Its detached 256-entry cue log includes missing and locale-suppressed requests. World audio stops on leaving the world; shutdown cancels fades and releases streams. Tests wait for the intentional menu fade. The initial shutdown resource messages are resolved in the final runs.

AudioDirector follows actual area, weather, raw night state and festival state; generated-floor music remains owned by DungeonCues. Shops ring on entry/exit, closed doors knock, local shops warn before closing, festivals duck ambience, and city bells are deduplicated per day/hour. Real equipment changes emit a generic event after the inventory transaction, so equipping the same weapon again is silent. Footsteps use actual movement and animation, builder surfaces first, bounded variation and no consecutive repeated variant. Surface lookup is performed only when a step is due. A floating-point remainder defect that doubled some running steps was corrected.

All fourteen original MP3s are preserved byte-for-byte. Eleven frozen lines are converted without text/pitch edits. The manifest covers 296 unique paths: eleven human files, 175 local Sara placeholders and 110 explicitly deferred draft social lines. Two families reused voice paths for different text (kin-look variants and seven midnight stories); those paths are now unique without changing the copy. The generator checks text/parameter/audio hashes, preserves protected human files, removes AIFFs, uses atomic exports and defers draft social copy. The later copy package must review and bake those 110 rows before final acceptance.

The final broader headless run passes 1,534 checks across eight sections with two existing stale-content skips. Eleven provoked negative-fixture messages are explicitly allowed; no unallowed engine errors, warnings or shutdown resource messages occur. The six independent Python tests pass: pitch duration, preset jitter bounds, original preservation, actual generator idempotency, deterministic PCM and decoded waveform checks. All 186 shipped voice files are also decoded and checked for non-silent, unclipped, bounded-duration speech. The 44 SFX, nine 24-second ambience loops and fourteen 47-second music placeholders total 2.309 MiB. `waveforms.json` records decoded duration, peak, RMS and loop seam measurements. This is numeric QA, not a listening review.

The final native run passes 347 checks with no skipped checks, engine errors/warnings or shutdown messages. It verifies the same playback hooks and the real mixer.
