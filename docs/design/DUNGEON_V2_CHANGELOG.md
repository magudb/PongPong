## 2. Where the fix differs from the reviewer's proposal, and why

* **9, fallback**: the reviewer asked for a scripted template with ONE fixed nook. Floors 5, 6, 9 and 11 need THREE nooks (spots + anchor + hidden main chest), so a template that satisfies the 46 invariants would be a second generator. Pinned seeds are the ordinary generator on a verified seed: every invariant holds by measurement (12 of 12 specs, 797 checks) instead of by construction, and the one real failure of 20 016 floors is covered. The reviewer's second option (nook-less template with exempted invariants) was rejected because the floor would lose its unique spots that day.
* **4, cap**: the reviewer's measured examples were 2 000 / 2 600 on chest-like sources. The editor measured a grid including 3 200 and 4 000; 3 200 keeps the deep rares (79 % / 48 %) and holds the income at the farm's rate at luck 0. The dial stays two data keys.
* **23, appraisal host**: F4's LootService instead of a P07 lead patch, because it needs no edit of two pinned tests and no built file.
* **27, V4 data**: moved to V3 (the reviewer's first fix) rather than listing three SKIP couplings; V3 grows to L.
* **6, origin uid**: declined (see row 6).
* **13, rounding**: tolerance rule rather than data change (the pinned table is the contract).
* **11, light check**: 14 faint lights instead of 15 (the reviewer's number fails; the measurement is in the ADDENDUM).
* **29, waves**: DL6 moved to wave 6 (new, not proposed); DL3 stays in wave 5 (it is where the real-player wiring is proven).
