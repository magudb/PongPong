# OPEN QUESTIONS - ghost addendum (only decisions the USER can make; each has a default that is already built)

1. **GQ1 - Is the look right?** The ghost has big DARK eyes with a white glint and no white of the eye (the other twelve figures have a white sclera); Bu is pale sky-blue-white, Hviske pale lilac-pink. Look at `proto/contact_sheet_ghost.png`, `proto/gamezoom_check.png`, `proto/sketch_vs_baked.png`. *Default:* dark eyes (cuter and more ghostly in the prototype). Switching to the family eye is one palette entry and a 13 second re-bake of both heroes.
2. **GQ2 - The names.** Race "Spøgelse", looks "Bu" (boo) and "Hviske" (whisper): the lead's defaults, kept because they are what a child expects. Keep them? *Default:* keep.
3. **GQ3 - Should the ghost be allowed to float over water** (a real perk: the maps would have to allow it) instead of only LOOKING floaty? *Default:* no; the fairy keeps the flight perk, the ghost's perk is the dark and cheaper running.
4. **GQ4 - Other ghosts as citizens** of Trætoppen / Hyggebyen (e.g. a ghost baker's apprentice)? *Default:* no; the player is the only ghost (the look cap per area is 16 and the hero-twin rule would need two more twins).
