Trætoppen reusable asset repair prompt
Use case: style-transfer / precise-object-edit

Target: [asset ID and source file]
Role: [world object / furniture turnaround / inventory icon / terrain / backdrop]
Native contract: [canvas, pivot, footprint, projection, number of rows/columns,
rotation order, growth stage or animation state, and separate light/float layers]

Reference roles:
1. tools/art/drawn/sources/heroes.png: character proportions and line character.
2. tools/art/drawn/sources/terrain.png: ground pigments and painted grain.
3. tools/art/drawn/sources/nature.png: vegetation and outdoor materials.
4. tools/art/drawn/sources/interior_domestic.png (or interior_regal.png): furnishings.
5. The target image: preserve its identity, functional parts and native contract.

Repaint the target in our chosen matte storybook gouache/watercolor style with
fine, slightly uneven warm grey-brown pencil contours. Preserve handmade grain,
layered leaves, worn honey timber, cream, moss/sage, quiet lavender and small brass
details. Shapes should be organic and readable next to the approved characters
and terrain. Preserve the object and its rarity silhouette: a mushroom canopy,
snail chest, root throne or moon bed must remain that specific design.

Keep neutral daytime material colors. Farm: moss, honey, bark and sky. Town:
lavender, rose, gold and chalk. Forest: night blue #1c2138, shadow moss #2f4a3f,
quiet teal #6fd6c4 and owl gold #f2c14e. These are pigment families, not flat fills.
UI inventory outlines are exported separately in ink #2b2340, with a pale
#e4eff5 night rim for legendary icons. Parchment/ink/gold belong to the UI theme.

For isometric objects use the fixed elevated orthographic 36-degree camera;
verticals stay upright and native ground tiles are 128 by 76. For an orthographic
asset preserve its orthographic projection instead. Do not change collision,
footprint, interaction or sorting data to accommodate a different picture.

For turnarounds, one object per row, four columns: r0 front bottom-left; r1 front
bottom-right; r2 back bottom-left; r3 back bottom-right. Turn the same physical
object. The rear must conceal handles, cushions or keys that are occluded. Check
that column two actually turns, rather than repeating column one. Wall objects
use the two front-facing wall orientations. Never silently mirror asymmetric
handles, leaf arrangements, embroidery or grain.

Use true alpha transparency for cutouts. Leave generous clear gutters on every
side of each complete silhouette. Separate crops must not touch. Keep all legs,
leaves, antennae, roof edges and accessories inside the assigned cell. No text,
labels, grid, room floor, ground rectangle, cast shadows or baked glow. Transparent
gutters must stay transparent; do not substitute a brown background wash.

Avoid glossy 3D rendering, plastic, low-poly balls/cylinders, vector cubes,
photorealism, thick black outlines and neon bloom. Do not add characters or
unrequested decorations. Preserve material and object identity: a berry pickup
is fruit, not a bush; metal buckets, stone hearths and cloth cushions retain their
own materials. Backdrops keep atmospheric depth and lower contrast than actors.

Export and review:
- Save the complete prompt and reference roles alongside the selected master.
- Extract the saved painting through tools/art/drawn/consistency.py.
- Preserve source RGB/alpha; packing, material variants and projection are
  deterministic exports. Technical shadows, light masks and telegraphs retain
  their functional geometry and readability and are not treated as illustrations.
- Project floor textiles to their actual footprint: equal isometric bounding-box
  ratios do not mean that runners and rectangular rugs have the same shape.
- Rebuild window masks from the current painted windows and align their offsets.
- Review the actual Godot imports at native scale, on light and dark backgrounds.
- Run art_home, art_style_consistency, art_world_style, art_farm and ui_foundation;
  keep the existing pixel-anchor, distinctness, compression and memory checks.

Actual generation/edit prompts and selected source paths from this pass are in
the adjacent *prompts.json files. Generated with the built-in imagegen tool.
