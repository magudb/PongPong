Current full-library style audit — codex-review — 15 October 2026

The selected game art and the shop/festival/equipment art studies follow the
approved painted character-and-tile direction. This audit covers the present
library after the newer art packages, rather than relying on the old 4,656-file
report. The current rules are consolidated in docs/art/style-guide.txt, backed
by the original local Style Guide HTML and the accepted paintings.

Inventory and review scope

all eight fonts load. The scan has zero unclassified rasters and zero format or

not an automated aesthetic score.

The fresh engine catalog resolves 58 characters, 345 item IDs, 29 terrain IDs

The production packer, optimizer and legacy environment/city install wrappers
