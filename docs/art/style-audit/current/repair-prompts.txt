Historical fixture repair recipe

Target list: tools/art/drawn/fixture_recipes.json (all 298 paths).
Drawing references: each entry's source; characters/terrain remain authoritative.
Instruction: replace the old CG/prototype drawing with that approved painting.
Keep historical filenames, tile cells, cutout canvas and ground anchor. Fit a
different source canvas uniformly around its measured ground pivot; do not warp
the character-facing proportions. Use the painted city hut's trimmed window
mask and matching offset rather than fitting its lights into an old rectangle.
Copy terrain variants cell for cell; keep all four water phases. Use the painted
window/rune masks and painted forest-glow material, not the old neon tile fills.
Keep neutral radial light kernels functional. Backdrop foliage must repeat at
the edges and leave transparent sky above its canopy. Doormat and timber parts
reuse accepted textile/terrain pigments. Export losslessly at the fixture path.

Reproduce: tools/art/.venv/bin/python tools/art/drawn/pack.py --only=fixtures
Verify: tools/art/.venv/bin/python tools/art/drawn/fixtures.py --check
The geometry archive contains only channel sums/alpha for joints and silhouettes;
it prevents repeated exports from compounding painted shading. It is not an art
reference. The production packer and optimizer restore these fixtures last.

Reusable future correction

Use case: precise-object-edit / identity-preserve / style-transfer.
Target: [master file, asset ID, cell/direction/frame and actual native capture].
Image 1: the exact target master; retain object identity and all unaffected cells.
Image 2: approved heroes.png or that hero's accepted body; proportions/contour.
Image 3: terrain.png or the matching selected furnishing master; material grain.
Problem: [specific contour/material/perspective/alpha/occlusion mismatch].
Change only: [the visible defect]. Preserve [canvas, pivot, footprint, projection,
rotation order, object parts, source spacing, hand identity and local palette].

Match matte storybook gouache/watercolor with subtle paper/pigment grain and
thin, slightly uneven warm pencil edges. Honey timber, sage/cream linen, quiet
lavender/terracotta, small dull brass and restrained iron. Keep the original
object's functional silhouette and rarity identity. No glossy/plastic/faceted
3D rendering, photoreal texture pasted onto a flat shape, thick black strokes,
vector-looking world objects or broad neon bloom. Turn the same physical object;
rear views hide front-only buttons, handles, glass contents and decorations.
Never silently mirror asymmetric texture, hand position or occluder order.

True alpha transparency; complete silhouettes and clear gutters. No floor card,
cast shadow, cell fill, guide grid, baked lighting wash or labels in cutouts.
Technical light/shadow/telegraph masks retain their functional native design;
do not paint them as furniture. UI vectors remain editable vectors in the theme
system, with parchment, ink and fairy gold. Caprasimo controls stay >=20 px.

For equipment preserve the safe body, native 256x256 canvas and (128,200) ground
anchor. Clothing, grips and hands must share the actual pose. Keep ears, hair,
wings, tail and ghost hem clear. Empty garment masters contain no character
parts. Do not give ghosts human legs or tint cloth as if it were ghost skin.
Do not present flattened concepts or missing clips as completed fitted layers.

Save the exact prompt, reference roles and a non-destructive corrected master
under the relevant package. Repack from source using that package's exporter;
keep transforms, trim offsets, material masks and light count/memory limits.
Review in Godot beside the actual heroes/tiles, on light/dark floors and in
appropriate day/evening states. Update the per-file review evidence only after
inspection, then run style_audit_current.py --check and relevant art tests.
