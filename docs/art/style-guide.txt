Trætoppen — asset style rules

The visual reference is docs/Trætoppen Style Guide Review/Trætoppen Style Guide.dc.html,
its local rendered references, and the accepted character/terrain paintings in
tools/art/drawn/sources/. The chosen direction is hand-painted storybook 2D:
matte gouache/watercolor, visible pigment/paper/wood grain, slightly irregular
warm pencil contours, friendly chibi silhouettes and readable functional parts.
Old soft-CG/geometry bake instructions describe historical prototypes and do not
replace this direction. Use the approved paintings as references when fixing art.

Characters and objects

Preserve each hero's head/body proportions, face, ears, hair, wings, tail and
material identity. Keep natural warm contours rather than glossy plastic or
uniform thick black strokes. Furniture and props use honey timber, woven cream
linen, sage, muted lavender, terracotta, restrained iron and small dull brass.
Keep distinct materials and rarity silhouettes: worn timber is not glossy metal;
a mushroom, snail chest or root throne must remain recognizable as that object.
Avoid photorealism, vector-looking world props, low-poly/faceted geometry and
unmotivated neon bloom. Do not repaint correctly matched art merely to change it.

Zone pigments and lighting

Farm: moss/sage, honey/bark, cream and open sky. Town: lavender, rose, chalk and
small warm gold details. Dark forest: night blue #1c2138, shadow moss #2f4a3f,
quiet teal #6fd6c4 and owl gold #f2c14e. These are pigment families and light
relationships, not a command to flatten every texture to a hex color. Backdrops
are softer and lower contrast than actors. Keep a legible character contour on
both light/dark floors. Review daytime and evening together; the forest remains
dark by design, with local accents rather than broad neon washes.

Native geometry and variants

Respect the existing projection and placement contract. Native isometric tiles
are 128x76; verticals stay upright. Preserve each object's canvas, footprint,
pivot, mount height, ground contact, interaction anchor and sorting data. A
wall, floor textile and free-standing object need different placement handling.
Do not stretch a floor runner to a diamond just because its bounding box fits.

New prop rotations must show the same physical object turning, including
credible backs and concealed front-only handles/buttons. No silent mirrored
asymmetry. Existing stock character mirrors are declared and reviewed as that
pack's contract; fitted handed equipment requires explicit directional grips
and occluders. Preserve cutout alpha and generous clear source gutters. Do not
bake a floor rectangle, cast glow or unwanted shadow into an illustrated sprite.
Keep terrain periods continuous and tile atlas mipmaps off.

Fitted equipment uses the existing 256x256 body canvas and ground anchor
(128,200). Clothing, hands, hair/ears, wings and tails must stay registered to the
same body pose. Keep safe base coverage when clothes are removed. Ghost trousers
and shoes become hem bands/tabs, never human legs. Cloth stays its own material.
Do not present a flattened concept or incomplete clip set as production clothing.

UI assets and typography

The UI stays parchment #f4e6c4 / cream #fbf5e6, ink #2b2340 and fairy gold
#e3c372 across all zones. UI symbols remain simple theme vectors; they do not
need the detail of a world painting. White mask symbols are tinted by their
actual control. Buttons have depth and visible gold focus. Inventory silhouettes
need readable ink edges; legendary night icons retain their pale #e4eff5 rim.

Caprasimo is the display/numeral font and is never below 20 px in controls.
Alegreya Sans is the body/dialogue font, with Danish Æ Ø Å at 18 px or larger.
Small developer asset IDs, contact-sheet labels and diagnostic annotations are
inspection aids, not the game's dialogue or control typography. Bundled symbol
and monospace fonts keep their specific UI/inspection roles and license files.

Explicit non-illustration roles

Shadows, contact masks, light/halo masks, telegraphs, mist and small readability
particles are technical layers. Review their contrast, placement and effect in
context; do not turn them into textured furniture or scene paintings. Keep the
existing light, compression and memory limits for each kit.

Historical generator/regression fixture paths must follow the painted style too.
Their explicit recipes are in tools/art/drawn/fixture_recipes.json; approved
masters supply the drawings without moving their canvas or ground anchor.
Trimmed city window masks use the painted windows' own dimensions and offsets.
Original channel sums and alpha measurements are manufacturing geometry only,
stored outside assets/; never use them as illustration references. Functional
radial light kernels remain technical layers. Concepts and art-only kits retain
their declared production status rather than claiming finished gameplay.

Review and repair

Inventory every assets/ file and resolve the actual game catalogs, night/base
icon variants, secondary masks and outdoor parallax. Decode every raster and
check JSON/SVG/font validity. Inspect changed/new art against the accepted source
paintings, including rotations, alpha edges, material identity and native scale.
Byte-identical earlier exports may keep their earlier recorded visual evidence;
record that reuse explicitly. A hash match or path name alone is not an aesthetic
judgment. Native light/dark, day/evening and character-adjacent previews remain
part of the review. For animation, name the exact sampled/all-frame scope.

Save exact generation/edit prompts, reference roles, selected masters and repair
instructions in docs/art/<package>/. Rebuild from saved sources rather than
editing lossy exports by hand. Run the relevant native and regression checks.
The reviewed snapshot detects additions/replacements; it never grants automatic
aesthetic approval just because a file decodes or has a painted-looking filename.
