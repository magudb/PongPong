# Painted farm seasons

Nineteen paintings complete the seasonal farm kit: twelve props, four particle images and three apple-tree variants. The existing summer tree remains unchanged. Cream blossoms, warm timber, muted rose, sage, ochre and lavender shadows follow the approved painted environment references. The tree variants retain the knotted trunk, roots and camera of the summer tree; the winter silhouette has bare branches and restrained snow.

Exact prompts and reference roles are saved in `prompts.json`. `generation-record.json` records all original built-in imagegen paths. All nineteen retained PNG masters are byte-identical to those outputs. The packer only trims alpha, uniformly contains paintings within their cells and losslessly encodes WebP; it adds no painted content or recolouring. `packing.json` records source hashes and export geometry. Decoded RGBA cost is 2.358 MiB, below the 4 MiB kit budget.

All full-size masters, the packed contact sheet and both native catalog captures were opened and inspected. The seven additional native farm captures show spring flowers/petals, summer sunflowers, autumn leaves/rain, winter snow, midnight fireflies, tier-ten arch/fountain/banners and real festival bunting. They fit beside the approved characters, terrain, lamps and homes. The particle gallery enlarges the tiny images four times for diagnosis; the world captures use actual game scale. The explicit ledger accepts only these nineteen new WebPs; all 2,906 prior entries remain byte-identical. This is the implementing agent's visual review.
