To the towns/interiors implementation owner

Consume assets/iso_props/shops/special/shops.json through the existing
PieceCatalog kit_manifest_paths. The 69 IDs retain the published I4 footprint,
layer, template, set and baked rotation list. Each rot entry supplies relative
file, native size, pivot, footprint, ysort, illustration and flip=false.
Keep core and specialist lookup priority above generic furniture: some IDs such
as potion_shelf have older domestic furniture with the same name.

Wall pivots use the first wall cell's edge midpoint, like the core kit and
InteriorRoom. Respect the supplied mounting height/pivot; do not centre a
multi-cell shelf using its bounding box. Tabletop and ceiling sprites retain a
ground anchor with their elevation baked into pivot.y; do not raise them twice.
Tabletop ysort=[0,19] sorts the accessory just after its support while retaining
its pixel position. Only floor pieces receive floor collision. Ceiling pieces
need their own foreground plane so actors cannot sort above a hanging object.
The art gallery demonstrates this with z_index=2; use the appropriate existing
runtime plane rather than importing the gallery.

night_offset and glow fields describe trimmed additive sprites. night_offset
is relative to the full body image's top-left. Use body offset + night_offset
once; these accents have light_hint=none and point_lights=0. No additional
PointLight2D is needed. The core fire masks retain their separate one-light cap.

time-of-day fading and ceiling ordering remain runtime owner work.
