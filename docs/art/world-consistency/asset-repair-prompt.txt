Reusable asset correction prompt — chosen painted style

Use with the built-in imagegen editor. Attach the existing asset and the relevant
approved source paintings before submitting the prompt below. Paths are relative
to the repository root. Save the edited master and the final prompt with its
reference roles, then export it through the native packer and inspect it in Godot.

Reference selection

Character style / proportions: tools/art/drawn/sources/heroes.png, plus the
specific character's existing master listed in tools/art/drawn/pack.py.
Buildings / warm windows: tools/art/drawn/sources/buildings.png.
Organic foliage / props: tools/art/drawn/sources/nature.png.
Tile pigments / painted materials: tools/art/drawn/sources/terrain.png.
Current environment masters: city_painted.png, city_details_painted.png and
forest_painted.png in tools/art/drawn/sources/.

Prompt template

Use case: illustration-story / style-transfer. Edit the attached EXISTING game
asset [ASSET ID / TARGET IMAGE] so it belongs beside our approved characters,
buildings and tiles. The attached approved paintings are the authoritative style
references. The existing asset is a shape, footprint and camera reference; its
old rendering style is not authoritative.

Match the approved hand-painted storybook gouache / watercolor: gently uneven
warm grey-brown pencil contours, matte painted pigments, subtle paper tooth,
organic worn materials, delicate moss and plants, restrained earthy olive / sage /
honey / muted lavender colours, and clear readable silhouettes. Characters retain
their approved rounded proportions, simple tiny eyes and existing identity. Match
the reference's detail density; keep focal shapes readable at the game's low zoom.
Use the tile master for timber grain, bark, stone and forest litter instead of
introducing a different material language. Preserve warm amber windows and cool
teal forest accents where appropriate.

Preserve [EXACT CANVAS DIMENSIONS], [PIVOT / GROUND ANCHOR], [FOOTPRINT], the
authored camera and the complete silhouette. The camera is elevated about
36 degrees, orthographic, with ground ellipses about .58 as tall as wide; match
the actual target's orientation. Keep interaction points, doors, openings, water
anchors and night-light positions compatible with the target. If a night mask
changes, derive it from the final painting and update its native offset.

Change only [REQUESTED CORRECTION]. Keep all other approved features. Render
neutral readable albedo: the engine supplies day/night grading, shadows and bloom.
Retain true transparency and anti-aliased organic edges. No ground rectangle,
paper background, cropped foliage, baked halo, labels, characters added to props,
glossy 3D surfaces, smooth geometric blobs, vector icons or photorealism.

For a sheet, use [ROWS x COLUMNS] separate complete alpha islands in the exact
listed order, generous transparent gutters and no overlap. Avoid detached tiny
pieces that the packer's island extraction could confuse with another asset.

Delivery: an edited transparent raster master at [OUTPUT PATH]. After packing,
inspect it beside the approved fox and tiles in the real city / forest at zoom
.2, .4 and a close view, including night. Check silhouette edges, window alignment,
sorting, visibility, bridge joins, and the existing texture budget.

Exact prompts used for this correction are in prompts.json and detail-prompt.txt
beside this template. They record the actual inputs and instructions, rather than
the placeholders above.
