manifest.json. All authored directions and fitted coordinates are kept in

Only Rasmus (fox) and Pedro (ghost_bu), four starter garments, iron sword, and

but become an under-hem band and leather tabs. No inventory mechanic changes.

Every region stores rect in atlas pixels and canvas_offset in the original
256x256 body canvas. Draw at canvas_offset - (128,200). Group transforms follow
the actor's Sprite position/rotation/scale/modulate and offset-base_sprite_offset
once; attachments must not apply altitude or spin offset a second time. Grip
positions and their local bases are already deformed per frame in canvas space.
The proof sword is baked at its recorded right grip; do not socket the baked
rectangle again. A future separately packed attachment must retain its own
explicit grip pivot and apply that pivot only once.

Paintings were authored separately for all eight directions. Do not assume a
mirrored direction has the same handedness or occluder order. Hand/ear occluders
are extracted from the matching safe base painting. Far-side sword regions can
be entirely hidden by the body/tail in this relaxed pose. Dedicated extended
arm/hand poses are needed for the final attack and two-handed tools.

The native Figure is an art-studio consumer, not the production EquipmentVisuals
service. It deliberately uses an existing dressed IsoChibiSprite2D as the only
clock, hides its sprite while supported proof poses are present, and restores
the complete stock actor on other clips. It loads the four supported clip groups
at setup and releases them with the view. Do not copy this eager review loading
policy into a catalog-wide production presenter. In particular, do not leave
ResourceLoader.load_threaded_request calls uncollected on view exit.
