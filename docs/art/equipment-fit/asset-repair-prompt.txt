Reusable painted fitted-equipment repair prompt
Use case: precise-object-edit / identity-preserve.

Image 1 = exact existing hero/base turnaround (identity, pose, body proportions).
Image 2 = matching selected clothing/weapon master (materials, seams, orientation).
Image 3 = failed native fit capture, if useful; its grid/labels are review aids,
never artwork to paint into the exported sprite.

Edit target: [master path, row, column, direction and garment/body part].
Problem: [ear painted into hat / wrong rear view / sleeve covering paw / tail
clipped / ghost gained legs / grip floats / seam jumps / material mismatch].
Change only: [precise correction]. Preserve all other cells and generous alpha
spacing, character identity, matching dimensions and item identity. Keep the
original master and save a new sibling version. Update the selected source in
layout data only after reviewing all eight directions in the native studio.

Use our matte storybook gouache/watercolor with subtle paper grain and thin,
slightly uneven warm grey-brown pencil contour. Worn honey straw, sage ribbon,
cream linen, brown woven cloth/leather, modest dull brass and restrained iron.
No glossy/3D/pixel/vector treatment, thick black stroke, neon bloom, cast shadow,
cell wash, labels, text or background. True transparent alpha for every cutout.

Directions: S front, SW front-left, W left profile, NW rear-left, N exact back,
NE rear-right, E right profile, SE front-right. Rear shirts hide front buttons.
Draw the same physical garment turning, not eight unrelated inventory icons.
Author opposite sides; do not silently mirror an asymmetric seam or handed grip.
Empty clothing contains no mannequin, ears, paws, hands, legs or character skin.
Fox ear openings must be holes; actual ears remain on the body/ear layer.
Pedro's shirt is short/open-sided; trousers become a woven hem band and shoes
become two small hem tabs. Preserve his floating scalloped wisp and mittens,
never human legs under a sheet. Cloth stays opaque and separate from blue skin.

Native fit contract: 256x256 untrimmed canvas, ground anchor (128,200), shared
body/garment deformation, explicit per-pose grips/visibility/depth, matching hand
occluders. An atlas trim must keep its canvas offset. Keep sword grip pivot and
local orientation; no inventory-icon scaling to fake a held view. Never solve
an occlusion problem by raising a weapon above the entire room's depth plane.

Save the exact prompt/reference roles in prompts.json and preserve selected
source hashes. Repack with pack.py --only=equipment-fit. Review every affected
frame at world scale and close up on light and dark floors, with clothes on/off,
and with common spin, altitude and tint. Run art_equipment_fit and the existing
characters/equipment concept gates. A schema-valid rig alone is not visual QA.
