Prompt 1 Watermarked letter and a repaired lamp

Create a hand-painted storybook source sheet with two separate object studies:
a folded cream paper letter and a small, well-used forest lantern. Match the
attached approved paintings: matte gouache and watercolor, visible pigment and
paper grain, slightly irregular warm pencil contours, rounded functional parts,
quiet detail and clear silhouettes. Use a 1024x1024 source canvas with generous
clear gutters and a transparent background. Keep the letter and lantern fully
separate so each can be extracted without cutting through another object.

The letter is creased and damp along one edge, with a subtle open-circle
watermark. Leave the paper free of readable writing; localized handwriting and
journal text are added by the game. The lantern has dull brass, a worn honey
timber handle, clear glass and a narrow cloth grip repair with three diagonal
cream stitches. Its small warm light should feel cared for rather than ornate.
Keep the functional handle, glass and base readable. The outer glow and contact
shadow are separate runtime layers, not painted rectangles behind the cutout.

This is a source study, not a drop-in sprite. Choose the final canvas, pivot,
interaction footprint and icon crop against the existing catalog contracts
before exporting. Do not introduce a mother portrait or species-specific hand.

Prompt 2 The clearing that remembers a room

Create one 1536x1024 environment concept in the attached game's painted forest
style. A small clearing holds the arrangement of a kitchen whose walls have
quietly disappeared: a worn wooden table, two simple mismatched chairs, a sewing
basket, an unfinished folded map and the repaired lantern from the approved
letter/lamp source study. Do not generate this before that study is selected;
attach its selected master as an additional identity reference.

Keep a broad readable approach and exit. The lantern gives a small patch of
owl-gold light on the table. Beyond it are shadow-moss trunks, night-blue gaps
and restrained quiet-teal mushrooms. A chair is pulled out as though somebody
has just stood up. The scene feels familiar and unsettling through absence and
arrangement. Keep the tactile timber, matte pigment and gentle contours of the
approved game. The clearing is not a realistic horror set. Avoid broad neon,
glossy surfaces and a black wash that hides paths. No people or readable text.

This is a composition concept, not a replacement floor texture or proof that a
story interior is implemented. After selection, separate new props and lighting
layers under the project's existing alpha, projection and sorting contracts.

Prompt 3 Roots that frame an unfinished tale

Create a 1024x1024 painted source study of an old wooden waymarker with roots
curling around its edges like an unfinished page frame. Match the attached
approved forest's viewing angle and materials. Keep the upright post, peg and
wood grain believable and the sign's silhouette readable beside the existing
chibi heroes. One strip of pale, fibrous bark is caught on a root like paper.
Leave the sign face blank for localized labels. Use warm pencil contours,
shadow moss and night blue, with a tiny dull-gold catchlight. Transparent
background, generous clear margins, no ground rectangle or baked light bloom.

The Root Scribe's presence is suggested by the repeated frame shape and an
empty place for a name. Do not add a character or resolve its final form in
this prop. Export only after its placement, collision, pivot and sorting have
been checked against the actual forest scene. Keep the existing navigation
widths and never conceal a clue interaction point beneath these roots.

Review before production use

Save the exact submitted prompts, reference roles and selected lossless masters
here when generation happens. Compare props beside every hero species in the
dark forest, at native scale and with the normal lantern. Inspect alpha edges,
the cream stitch identity, ground contact, path clearance, occlusion and text
contrast. New paintings need an explicit style review and the usual asset,
import and memory checks. The current approval ledger covers existing assets,
not these ungenerated requests.
